# HI-Tech Website — Case Study in Progress

**This is a working outline, not the final showcase.** Phase 1 is archived and Phase 2 research is in progress. Update this document with verified decisions and evidence as the project develops; polish it into the final narrative after validation.

## Business problem

Explain HI-Tech Software Consultancy's custom business-software services to retailers, wholesalers, and manufacturers, establish trust through its experience, and make consultation easy through phone and WhatsApp.

## Design approach established so far

The approved direction uses IBM Plex Sans, a 12-column grid, generous whitespace, a restrained red accent, Gestalt grouping, and deliberate motion. The project owner directs the process through the business brief, reference selection, Canva annotations, and reviews. AI tools assist with research, interpretation, code, imagery, and verification.
